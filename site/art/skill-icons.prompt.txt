Skill icons for a pixel-art fighting game: a night duel between a gold Thunder cultivator and an ice-blue Frost cultivator. Its HUD has bronze frames, chunky square pixels and a dark night-purple field.

Make one sheet of 25 icons in a strict grid of 5 columns and 5 rows on a plain black (#000000) background, with a wide black gap between icons. Each icon is a square drawn on a 32 by 32 grid of hard-edged, perfectly square pixels (every pixel the same size, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no soft glow), so it reads clearly at 32 x 32 pixels.

Each icon: a dark night-purple (#1a1424) square field, and on it ONE bold, simple, centred silhouette that fills most of the field, a 1-pixel dark outline round it, and only 3 shades of one colour plus white for the highlight. No small details, no scenery, no text, numbers or letters, no frame or border.

Gold icons use #6a4410, #e8b030, #ffd84a and white. Blue icons use #2c58aa, #62c8f4, #a6ecff and white.
