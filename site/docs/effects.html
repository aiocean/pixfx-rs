Effect catalog

pixfx keeps only effects that read the sprite they play on, or the way it moved. Each takes the common options seed, flip, angle and light; see Options.

An effect that reads neither, such as a fire, an explosion or a shield, is not an engine effect: it is sprite art the game plays. Open any effect below in the effects gallery to see it live, with its size, origin, options and tags as the engine reports them.

EffectReadsWhat it does
explosions/body-burstthe spriteThe character blows apart into chunks of its own sprite that crack smaller as they fly, down to dust. The game removes the sprite when it starts.
feedback/outlinethe spriteA ring round the sprite it wraps, following each frame: to read a character over busy ground, or to show what is picked.
feedback/power-upthe spriteA buff: streaks drawn in, the character flashing, then an aura gripping its outline and licking upward.
projectiles/trailits motionThe streak a mover leaves along the way it came, longer the faster it went. Played on a character it smears that body's back edge.

The appear and disappear categories are empty for now: they hold effects that read the sprite as it enters or leaves the scene.
