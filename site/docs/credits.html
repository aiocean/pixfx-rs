Projects it builds on

  • pixelfx, Copyright (c) 2026 Patch, Copyright (c) 2026 Hugo Duprez, MIT License (github.com/patch-gg/pixelfx, commit 044e12a). The effects and the engine they run on are ports of pixelfx.
  • beanfx, Copyright (c) 2026 bien, MIT License (github.com/baenv/beanfx). The sprite sheets use the beanfx layout (three PNGs and Aseprite-style JSON with a meta.beanfx block) and its rule for compositing light over transparency.

pixelfx notices

Hugo Duprez. pixelfx's src/core/sim.js and the effects that say so at the top of their file adapt Sprite Fusion's open-source pixel art VFX (spritefusion.com/pixel-art-vfx), Copyright (c) 2026 Hugo Duprez, MIT License. In pixfx-rs, the fire effects' flame tongues, licks, flame puffs, smoke, embers and sparks come from this work.

Hollow Crown. pixelfx's src/core/act.js adapts how Hollow Crown plays its skills, from github.com/rehan-remade/hollow-crown, Copyright (c) 2026 rehan-remade, MIT License.

Patch Pixel. The font in pixelfx's gallery/fonts is Patch's own and is not covered by pixelfx's MIT license. pixfx-rs does not include it.
